## 1. Business Background
The traditional recruitment and skill assessment processes face significant challenges in the modern, fast-paced digital economy. Employers and candidates encounter mutual friction that reduces the efficiency of human capital allocation.

* **Current recruitment problems:** High volume of unqualified applicants clogs the initial screening pipeline, causing recruiters to spend disproportionate time filtering rather than engaging with top talent.
* **Current interview problems:** Scheduling conflicts, interviewer unavailability, and inconsistent interview formats lead to a prolonged and fragmented process.
* **Current training problems:** Candidates lack targeted, actionable guidance on which skills they need to improve to meet market demands.
* **Manual assessment limitations:** Human-led technical assessments are difficult to scale, often relying on rudimentary take-home assignments or unstructured whiteboard sessions.
* **Cost of current process:** Organizations incur heavy costs in man-hours spent on pre-screening, initial interviews, and travel/logistics for on-site assessments.
* **Time consumption:** The end-to-end recruitment cycle from application to offer often exceeds 30-45 days, risking candidate drop-off.
* **Human bias:** Unconscious biases regarding a candidate's background, appearance, or communication style frequently compromise the objectivity of evaluations.
* **Lack of objective evaluation:** Evaluation criteria vary wildly between different human interviewers, even within the same department.
* **Candidate experience issues:** Candidates often wait weeks for feedback, or receive generic rejection emails without understanding their specific skill gaps.
* **Employer pain points:** High drop-out rates in the pipeline, bad hires due to inaccurate skill assessment, and burned-out HR personnel.

## 2. Business Vision
* **Long-term vision:** To become the industry standard ecosystem where talent preparation directly meets employer expectation through a unified, unbiased, and AI-driven platform.
* **Digital transformation goals:** Shift recruitment from a manual, heuristic-based approach to a scalable, automated, and quantifiable digital pipeline.
* **AI-driven assessment strategy:** Utilize advanced artificial intelligence to conduct dynamic, context-aware interviews that adapt to candidate responses in real-time.
* **Continuous learning ecosystem:** Bridge the gap between assessment and learning by providing personalized, AI-generated skill development roadmaps based on interview performance.
* **Standardized interview process:** Ensure every candidate is evaluated against the exact same baseline criteria, eliminating subjective variance.
* **Data-driven hiring:** Empower organizations to make hiring decisions based on comprehensive, granular data regarding both technical competencies and soft skills.

## 11. Business Rules (High-Level)
* **BRule-001:** A Candidate must upload a valid CV or manually complete their profile before attempting a formal employer assessment.
* **BRule-002:** A Candidate must complete at least one mock interview before the system will generate a personalized learning roadmap.
* **BRule-003:** Only Paid/Enterprise Employer accounts can unlock custom branding and AI question generation capabilities.
* **BRule-004:** Candidate global ranking is only updated after a formal assessment is fully processed and scored by the AI engine.
* **BRule-005:** Roadmap generation algorithms depend strictly on the data outputs from the most recently completed assessments.
* **BRule-006:** A formal interview session cannot commence without successful completion of the hardware check (Microphone/Camera).
* **BRule-007:** Payment gateways must return a "Success" webhook before a user is granted access to premium tier features.
* **BRule-008:** Cheating flags generated by the AI do not automatically disqualify a candidate; they mark the profile for mandatory human review.

## 12. Success Metrics
* **Average interview duration:** Tracked against the baseline goal of <30 mins per screening.
* **Average assessment completion rate:** Target >85% of started assessments are completed without drop-off.
* **Practice completion rate:** Target >60% of registered candidates complete at least 2 practice sessions.
* **Roadmap generation rate:** Percentage of candidates utilizing the roadmap feature.
* **Employer satisfaction:** Measured quarterly via NPS surveys on the employer portal.
* **Candidate satisfaction:** Measured via post-assessment feedback prompts (Target 4.5/5 stars).
* **Revenue / Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** Growth of premium subscriptions.
* **Retention:** Active Monthly Users (MAU) for candidates not actively in a hiring cycle.
* **Conversion:** Trial-to-paid ratio for employers (Target >15%).
* **Accuracy:** Variance between AI scoring and manual Employer scoring overrides.
* **AI recommendation acceptance:** Percentage of AI-generated questions accepted by employers without edits.

* **BA-003:** Employers and Candidates are generally willing to accept AI as an initial screening tool.

## 15. Business Constraints
* **Budget:** Initial rollout must remain within the Capstone/Phase 1 funding limits.
* **Timeline:** Core system (MVP) must be deployed and operational by the end of Q2 2026.
* **AI Cost:** API calls to external LLMs (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude) must be optimized to prevent runaway operational costs.
* **Cloud infrastructure:** Must utilize cost-effective tiering; heavy video storage requires lifecycle management (auto-delete after 90 days).
* **Legal compliance:** Must adhere to Vietnamese labor and data protection regulations regarding candidate data.
* **Privacy:** PII must never be used to train baseline AI models without explicit, opt-in consent.
* **Browser compatibility:** The platform will officially support the latest versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox.
* **Platform limitations:** The initial release will be a Desktop-optimized Web Application; mobile browser support is secondary.

## 16. Dependencies
* **Authentication:** Depends on Google/Microsoft SSO APIs and internal JWT infrastructure.
* **Payment:** Depends on Stripe (or equivalent local gateway like VNPay) API availability.
* **Email:** Depends on AWS SES or SendGrid for high-deliverability transactional emails.
* **Storage:** Depends on AWS S3 for secure storage of CVs, images, and recorded video files.
* **AI Models:** Depends on external LLM availability and latency for generating dynamic questions and analyzing text.
* **Calendar:** Depends on Google Workspace / MS Graph APIs for scheduling integrations.
* **Video Recording:** Depends on WebRTC and browser-based MediaRecorder APIs.
* **Monitoring:** Depends on DataDog, Sentry, or equivalent for application health tracking.

## 17. Business Value Summary
* **For Candidate:** Empowers job seekers with a fair, unbiased platform to prove their skills, practice in a low-stakes environment, and receive actionable roadmaps to improve their career trajectory.
* **For Employer (Organization):** Drastically reduces the time and financial cost associated with top-of-funnel recruiting while ensuring a higher baseline of quality for interviewed candidates.
* **For Recruiter / HR:** Eliminates the manual drudgery of CV screening and repetitive initial phone screens, allowing focus on relationship building and closing top candidates.
* **For Management / Platform Owner:** Creates a highly scalable, data-rich SaaS product capable of monetizing both sides of the recruitment marketplace (B2B campaigns and B2C premium prep).
* **For Training Department:** Provides empirical data on aggregate skill deficiencies, allowing organizations to tailor internal training programs to actual needs.
