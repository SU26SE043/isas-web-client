# Enterprise Reporting Requirements Specification
## 1. Document Purpose
### 1.1 Purpose
This document defines the enterprise reporting, dashboarding, and analytics requirements for the AI-powered Interview & Skill Assessment System (ISAS). It outlines the business intelligence (BI) deliverables necessary to support decision-making, operational monitoring, and strategic planning across all user personas.

### 1.2 Scope
The scope includes all structured reports, executive dashboards, self-service BI capabilities, KPIs, embedded analytics, and compliance reporting generated by ISAS. It excludes the physical data warehouse architecture, ETL/ELT pipelines, database schemas, and source code implementations.

### 1.3 Intended Audience
*   **Executive Sponsors:** For strategic alignment and KPI monitoring.
*   **Product Owners & Business Analysts:** To validate reporting functionality.
*   **BI Architects & Data Engineers:** As a blueprint for logical data modeling and presentation layer design.
*   **QA/Testing Teams:** To define acceptance criteria for data validation.

### 1.4 Relationship with Business Requirements
Reports and dashboards defined herein directly satisfy the monitoring and measurement objectives established in the Business Requirements Document (BRD). Every report must trace back to a specific business process or goal.

### 1.5 Relationship with Data Requirements
This specification dictates the *consumption* needs, which in turn drive the logical data models, data retention policies, and data quality thresholds defined in the Data Requirements Specification.

### 1.6 Relationship with Functional Requirements
Functional requirements dictate *how* the system operates; these reporting requirements dictate how the outcomes of those operations are measured, tracked, and visualized within the application UI or external BI tools.
## 2. Reporting Strategy
The ISAS reporting strategy aligns with DAMA-DMBOK2 and enterprise BI standards, focusing on delivering actionable, timely, and accurate insights. 

### 2.1 Operational Reporting
Provides day-to-day transactional insights (e.g., daily completed interviews, active support tickets). Focused on current state and immediate actions.

### 2.2 Management Reporting
Focuses on short-to-medium-term trends (e.g., weekly recruitment pipeline, monthly learning completion rates) to enable managers to optimize processes.

### 2.3 Executive Dashboards
High-level, highly aggregated visualizations of critical KPIs (e.g., Customer Acquisition Cost, Overall Platform Health) designed for C-suite decision-making.

### 2.4 Analytical Reporting
Deep-dive capabilities allowing users to slice and dice data (e.g., candidate performance by demographic and skill group) to uncover hidden patterns.

### 2.5 Self-Service Reporting
Empowers enterprise employers and internal admins to create custom views and ad-hoc reports using predefined, governed semantic data models without IT intervention.

### 2.6 Compliance Reporting
Ensures adherence to legal and regulatory frameworks (e.g., GDPR data deletion logs, AI bias monitoring, accessibility compliance).

### 2.7 Audit Reporting
Tracks system access, data modifications, and security events for internal and external auditing purposes.

### 2.8 Historical Reporting
Maintains snapshot and slowly changing dimensions (SCD) to compare current performance against past periods (YoY, MoM).

### 2.9 Real-time Monitoring
Event-driven updates for critical system metrics (e.g., active concurrent interviews, payment gateway uptime).
## 3. Report Categories
Reports are logically grouped into the following business domains:
*   **Candidate Reports (CR):** Focus on candidate profiles, engagement, and progression.
*   **Employer Reports (ER):** Focus on employer usage, ROI, and hiring metrics.
*   **Recruitment Reports (RR):** Pipeline, funnel, and campaign metrics.
*   **Interview Reports (IR):** Specifics regarding interview execution, duration, and completion.
*   **AI Assessment Reports (AR):** Evaluation of AI scoring, bias, and technical accuracy.
*   **Learning Reports (LR):** Skill gap closures, course completions, and roadmap progress.
*   **Payment Reports (PR):** Transactions, refunds, and financial reconciliation.
*   **Subscription Reports (SR):** MRR, churn, upgrades, and tier distributions.
*   **Support Reports (SUP):** Ticketing, resolution times, and customer satisfaction.
*   **Administration Reports (ADM):** Platform usage, storage, and tenant management.
*   **Audit Reports (AUD):** Access logs, permission changes, and security events.
*   **Operational Reports (OPS):** System health, concurrent usage, and latency.
*   **Executive Reports (EXE):** Strategic summaries and high-level KPIs.
*   **Compliance Reports (CMP):** Privacy, data protection, and fairness audits.
*   **Analytics Reports (ANL):** Predictive trends and deep demographic analysis.

## 5. Detailed Report Specifications

### RPT-001: Candidate Profile Completeness
- **Business Objective:** Measure missing profile data
- **Description:** A detailed operational view providing insights into candidate profile completeness to enable Candidate Success to make data-driven decisions.
- **Target Users:** Candidate Success, BI Analysts, System Admins.
- **Required Filters:** Date Range, Tenant/Employer ID, Candidate Demographics, Status, Geographic Region.
- **Grouping:** By Month/Week, by Employer, by Skill Category.
- **Sorting:** Default descending by Date, optional by Metric Value.
- **Business Metrics:** Total Count, Average Value, MoM Growth %, Variance.
- **Business Calculations:** `(Current Period - Previous Period) / Previous Period * 100`
- **Drill-down Capability:** Yes (Drill from Aggregate -> Tenant -> Individual Record).
- **Export Options:** PDF, Excel, CSV.
- **Security Restrictions:** Strictly Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Row-level security applied by Tenant ID.
- **Refresh Frequency:** Weekly.
- **Related Business Rules:** BR-SEC-01 (Data Masking), BR-REP-02 (Timezone Normalization).
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Report loads in < 3 seconds. Totals match the operational database exact count for the period.

## 8. Analytics Requirements
Beyond standard reporting, ISAS requires advanced analytical capabilities:
*   **Trend Analysis:** Moving averages and exponential smoothing for hiring trends over quarters/years.
*   **Skill Gap Analysis:** Heatmaps identifying discrepancies between employer requirements and candidate market supply.
*   **Performance Comparison:** Benchmarking an employer's time-to-hire against industry standards.
*   **Cohort Analysis:** Tracking retention and success of candidate cohorts hired in specific periods.
*   **Predictive Indicators:** AI-driven forecasting for candidate drop-off risks and subscription churn probability.
*   **Data Lineage Visuals:** Visualizing how a candidate's baseline score improves through learning modules.

## 9. Filtering & Search
All reports and dashboards must support standardized global filters:
*   **Temporal:** Date Range (Today, WTD, MTD, YTD, Custom, Rolling 30/90 Days).
*   **Organizational:** Company, Department, Hiring Manager.
*   **Categorical:** Industry, Job Role, Interview Type, Subscription Tier.
*   **Demographic/Geographic:** Location, Language.
*   **Outcome-based:** Status (Hired, Rejected, Pending), Score Range, Skill.
*   **Functional capabilities:** Multi-select checkboxes, type-ahead search, pagination (50/100/500 rows), sorting on all column headers, and Saved Filters (user-level personalization).

## 10. Export Requirements
Users require the ability to extract data for external processing or presentations:
*   **Supported Formats:** PDF (WYSIWYG layout), Excel (.xlsx with raw data on Sheet 2), CSV (UTF-8 encoded for system ingest).
*   **Bulk Export:** Capability to export >100,000 rows asynchronously with email notification upon completion.
*   **Permissions:** Export buttons are strictly governed by RBAC (e.g., standard recruiters cannot export PII in bulk).
*   **Audit Logging:** Every export action logs the User ID, Timestamp, Report Name, Parameters, and IP address (BR-SEC-04).
*   **Share Link:** Generate a secure, time-expiring (e.g., 48-hour) URL to share a report snapshot with non-licensed stakeholders.

## 11. Scheduled Reports
The BI platform must support automated report bursting and scheduling:
*   **Frequencies:** Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly.
*   **Delivery Channels:** Email (PDF attachment or HTML body), SFTP, Slack/Teams webhook integration.
*   **Event-Triggered:** Alerts sent when a KPI breaches a threshold (e.g., Server CPU > 90%, Interview Completion Rate < 50%).
*   **Recipients:** Defined user groups, roles, or dynamic external email lists.
*   **Retention:** Generated scheduled report artifacts are retained in the BI portal for 90 days.

## 12. Report Security
*   **Row-Level Security (RLS):** Data is dynamically filtered so Employer A cannot see Employer B's data under any circumstances.
*   **PII Masking:** Personally Identifiable Information (Names, Emails, Phone Numbers) is masked (`J*** D**`) for analysts and non-privileged roles.
*   **Access Control:** Managed via SSO/SAML integration mapped to internal system roles.
*   **Confidential Reports:** Executive financial data and HR compliance reports require strict group-based access provisioning.

## 13. Compliance Reporting
To meet legal obligations (GDPR, CCPA, EEOC):
*   **Data Retention/Deletion Reports:** Proof of compliance with "Right to be Forgotten" requests.
*   **AI Fairness Audits:** Statistical disparate impact analysis required by AI hiring laws (e.g., NYC Local Law 144).
*   **Access Audits:** Regular reviews of who holds "Super Admin" privileges.

## 14. Reporting Performance Requirements
*   **Dashboard Load Time:** < 3 seconds for initial render (cached).
*   **Ad-hoc Query Time:** < 10 seconds for queries spanning < 1 year of data.
*   **Concurrent Users:** Must support 1,000 concurrent reporting users without degradation.
*   **Export Time:** < 5 seconds for < 10,000 rows; asynchronous background processing for larger sets.
*   **Availability SLA:** 99.9% uptime during business hours.

## 15. Data Freshness
*   **Real-time (0-1 min latency):** Operational dashboards, Support ticketing, System health metrics.
*   **Near Real-time (5-15 min latency):** Interview progress, Candidate pipeline status.
*   **Hourly:** Financial transactions, Credits usage.
*   **Daily (Batch processing):** AI Model training metrics, Aggregated KPIs, Cohort analytics.
*   **Static/Snapshot (Monthly):** Financial month-end close reports, Compliance snapshots.
